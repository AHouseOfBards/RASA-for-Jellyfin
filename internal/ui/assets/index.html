RASA for Jellyfin

    Let's make your server reachable from anywhere

    RASA gives your Jellyfin server its own web address and a proper security certificate, sets up your router, and tells Jellyfin about it. There are six steps and you only need to be at the keyboard for three of them. It usually takes about five minutes, though one step waits on a certificate authority and can occasionally take longer on its own. You'll be told when it needs you again.

    You've set this up before. Running setup again will check everything over and repair whatever needs it.

      Setup can't continue yet

      Sign in to Jellyfin

      This must be the account you set Jellyfin up with. RASA needs administrator access to change your server's network settings, and your password is sent only to your own server.

      In Jellyfin: Dashboard → API Keys → add a key.

      Create your free Dynu account

      Your web address comes from Dynu, who give them away free. The account is yours, not RASA's. Nobody else can change or take your address.

      1. Create a Dynu account. It asks for an email address and a password.
      2. Come back here and open the API key page. The second button below goes straight to it, so you don't have to find it in the menus.
      3. Copy the key and paste it in the box. RASA checks it as soon as you do.

      RASA stores this key on this computer only, protected by your operating system. It's what keeps your address pointing at your home connection after RASA is gone.

      Opening the door

      Your router can probably do this for you. Most routers have a setting called UPnP, and yours has it switched off. If you turn it on and press Test again, RASA can open the port itself and you can skip the steps below. It is usually under the router's advanced or network settings.
